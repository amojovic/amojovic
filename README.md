# Hi, I'm Aleksa.

I work primarily with **machine learning and its mathematical foundations**, with a particular interest in understanding the mathematics and mechanics behind different learning methods and applying them to problems across different domains. I'm especially interested in where these ideas can lead outside their usual applications, from **quantitative finance and information retrieval to scientific modeling and less conventional problems**.

One of my main goals is to make **large, capable models accessible on ordinary personal hardware**. I'm interested in techniques such as quantization and other ways of making models more efficient, with a particular focus on keeping powerful models practical and **open source**.

Outside of that, I've spent a lot of time on **game development**, and I still find myself periodically disappearing into **robotics and microcontrollers, especially Arduino**. I've also recently been getting into **biological computation**, particularly the idea of taking inspiration from analog neurons to build more efficient analog neural systems.

If you want to see some of the more fun and unusual things I make, I keep a little corner of the internet with a collection of **projects, experiments, interesting concepts, art, and occasional thoughts or ideas that haven't become projects yet**:

🌐 **[The Mraki Archive](https://amojovic.github.io/the-mraki-archive/)**
